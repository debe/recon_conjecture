## What the round learned

1. **Coset freedom; the main structural lesson.**
   - Let τ be any permutation that maps every vertex to one with an isomorphic card. Then G.comap τ is an aligned hypomorph.
   - It differs from G unless τ ∈ Aut(G).
   - So every "Δ = 0" class, DWC₁ included, lies inside TW: the graphs whose removal-similar vertices are twins.
   - Classes that want to go further must conclude K = πG for an explicit π, as WR already does.
2. **Colour refinement is not tied pointwise.** Main-session check of t1's example:
   - Edges: u0a0, u0b0, a0b0, a0u1, u1a1, u1b1, a1b1, a2b2, a2u0.
   - G − u0 and G − u1 are both a triangle with a pendant 2-path plus a disjoint edge, so σ = (u0 u1) is a self-hypomorphism.
   - σ is not an automorphism, since u0b0 is an edge and u1b0 is not.
   - u0 and u1 have different round-3 colours: u0 has a neighbour of a degree-1 vertex, and u1 does not.
   - A tied vertex function must be constant on removal-similar vertices. So the tree tie, RK(paw), CC1 and "Δ ⟂ stable cells" are all false. All are recorded as dead ends.
   - Paths survive only because of their end-swapping symmetry together with A = Aᵀ.
3. **The symmetric layer has a cohomological obstruction** (t2, PM).
   - Assume an involution τ with 𝒰(G) = Fix(τ).
   - Then every aligned hypomorph is G^Q: G with some of the perfect matchings between τ-pairs flipped.
   - G^Q ≅ G exactly when the class [Q] ∈ H¹(M_τ; ℤ₂) vanishes. This is a Cai–Fürer–Immerman-type twist.
   - So a forest M_τ implies reconstructible.
   - The open signed-cover problem (t2 §5.5): do the cards force [Q] = 0? It is finite-dimensional and purely combinatorial.
4. **Honest reach.** t2 itself says CT, WRT and PM may miss the hard part. Known members of the older residuals are prime with walk rank 2, and on that face CT collapses to the checked BS₄ class.
