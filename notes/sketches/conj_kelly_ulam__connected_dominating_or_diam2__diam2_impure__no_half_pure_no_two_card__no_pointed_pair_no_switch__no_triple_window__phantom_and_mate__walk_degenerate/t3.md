## 1. Setup and a correction of the goal

### 1.1 Notation

G on V, |V| = n ≥ 3, A = A_G, K an **aligned hypomorph**: a graph on V with card isomorphisms
f_v : K − v → G − v for every v (K.Adj x y ⇔ G.Adj (f_v x)(f_v y) for x, y ≠ v). A' = A_K,
Δ = A' − A (symmetric, zero diagonal, Δ_xy ∈ {0, −1} on edges of G and {0, 1} on non-edges).

Formal power series in t: N = (I − tA)⁻¹, C_v = N_vv (closed walks at v), w_v = (N1)_v (walks
from v), λ_v := w_v / C_v (a unit, constant term 1). The same with a superscript K, and with
(v) for the card G − v: C_x^{(v)}, w_x^{(v)} are the series of x in G − v. 𝒮(t) := N^K − N =
Σ_k t^k (A'^k − A^k) = tΔ + t²(AΔ + ΔA + Δ²) + … .

w_k = A^k 1, M = span{w_k} (main space), N-space = M^⊥ (written 𝒩 below to keep N for the
resolvent), 𝒟 = span of degree-class indicators, 𝒰 = M + 𝒟.
**Type** of a vertex: τ_x := (w_x, C_x). Checked Theorem H says τ_x^K = τ_x^G for every x.

Two different "balanced swaps" at a vertex v; the task text merges them, and they must be kept
apart:
- z_v := row v of Δ = 1_{N_K(v)} − 1_{N_G(v)}. It is orthogonal to 𝒰(**G**) (walk vectors and
  degree classes of G). It is **not** orthogonal to the card's walk vectors: against the
  degree vector of G − v it gives ⟨z_v, deg_G − 1_{N(v)}⟩ = +(number of neighbours lost at v).
- δ_v := 1_{f_v(N_K(v))} − 1_{N_G(v)} (card-aligned). It is orthogonal to 𝒰(**G − v**). This is
  the swap of WR and of the residual's last clause. δ_v = 0 iff f_v extends to K ≅ G.
- Relation (from deg_{K−v} x = deg_{G−v} f_v x): with d^{(v)} the degree in G − v,
  z_v(x) = d^{(v)}(x) − d^{(v)}(f_v x) and δ_v(y) = deg_G(f_v⁻¹y) − deg_G(y).
  So z_v is the card-degree displacement of f_v and δ_v is its G-degree displacement.

### 1.2 Lemma SH: self-hypomorphisms (paper)

Let 𝒫 be the partition of V into removal-similarity classes (x ~ x' iff G − x ≅ G − x') and
Sym(𝒫) its Young subgroup (permutations preserving every class).

**Lemma SH.**
1. For τ ∈ Sym(𝒫) the graph G^τ (G^τ.Adj x y ⇔ G.Adj τx τy) is an aligned hypomorph of G, and
   its Δ is zero iff τ ∈ Aut(G).
2. Every aligned hypomorph K has the same partition 𝒫, and Sym(𝒫) acts on the set ℋ(G) of
   aligned hypomorphs. **G is reconstructible iff ℋ(G) is the single orbit {G^τ}.**
3. If some argument yields Δ = 0 for every aligned hypomorph of G, then Sym(𝒫) ≤ Aut(G), i.e.
   G ∈ **TW**: any two removal-similar vertices are twins (N(x) ∖ x' = N(x') ∖ x).

Argument. 1: G^τ − v ≅ G − τv by τ, and G − τv ≅ G − v. Δ = P_τᵀAP_τ − A. 2: K − v ≅ G − v gives
the partition. If ψ : G → K is an isomorphism then G − v ≅ K − ψv ≅ G − ψv, so ψ ∈ Sym(𝒫) and
K = G^{ψ⁻¹}. 3: a transposition (x x') is an automorphism iff x, x' are twins. ∎

**The one explicit graph (hand verification).** P₄ = a–u–u'–b, τ = (u u'), K = G^τ with edges
au', u'u, ub. Cards: G − a and K − a are paths on three vertices; so are G − b, K − b;
G − u = {u'b} + a and K − u = {au'} + b; G − u' = {au} + b and K − u' = {ub} + a. So the identity
is a hypomorphism G → K. Δ loses au, u'b and gains au', ub:
Δ = (e_a − e_b)(e_{u'} − e_u)ᵀ + (e_{u'} − e_u)(e_a − e_b)ᵀ ≠ 0, and K ≅ G.
All conclusions of Theorem H hold (u, u' and a, b are similar). The walk matrix has rank 2
(M = span{1, deg}), 𝒩 = span{e_a − e_b, e_u − e_u'}, corank 2.

Consequences for this step.
- "Equality of off-diagonal walk counts for all pairs" is not a consequence of hypomorphy
  for reconstructible graphs. It is equivalent to "σ itself is an isomorphism".
- DWC₀, DWC₁ and the class ZD below conclude Δ = 0, so they lie inside TW. Round-14 t2's
  Proposition PS (a removal-similar non-twin pair forces corank ≥ 2) is the same fact seen
  from the other side.
- By round-14 t2 Lemma RS, e_x − e_x' ∈ 𝒩 for removal-similar x, x'. So M ⊆ ℝ^𝒫 (functions
  constant on card classes) and 𝒩 ⊇ 𝒫₀ := {f : Σ_{x∈P} f(x) = 0 for every class P}, with
  dim 𝒫₀ = n − |𝒫|. A graph with few card types has a large non-main space *for a reason that
  has nothing to do with non-reconstructibility*. The residual's two new clauses are
  satisfied by reconstructible-looking structure: "dim 𝒰^⊥ ≥ 2" by one removal-similar
  non-twin pair x, x' (e_x − e_x' and 1_{N(x)∖x'} − 1_{N(x')∖x} lie in 𝒰^⊥: Lemma RS, Proposition
  PS, and T1 applied to the self-hypomorphism (x x')), and "a nonzero balanced swap at every
  vertex" by any graph in which every vertex has a pseudo-similar mate (removal-similar, not
  similar) or a card automorphism moving N(v).
- Spectral form of the conjecture: for every aligned hypomorph the orthogonal matrix
  Q = I_M ⊕ Q_𝒩 of Theorem D can be chosen as a permutation matrix of Sym(𝒫). A necessary
  linear condition is ΠΔΠ = 0 for Π the projection onto ℝ^𝒫: the number of edges between two
  card classes is tied. I have no argument for it; it is the pair census of rounds 9–13
  (which pairs of card types are adjacent) in matrix form.

## 2. Theorem H in matrix form: the space 𝒵 and the class ZD

### 2.1 The space 𝒵

𝒵_M(G) := {S symmetric n × n : S_vv = 0 for all v, S w_k = 0 for all k};
𝒵_𝒰(G) := {S ∈ 𝒵_M : S 1_{deg = j} = 0 for all j}. Both are rational subspaces.

**Proposition Z (paper).** For an aligned hypomorph K:
1. A'^k − A^k ∈ 𝒵_M for every k, hence p(A') − p(A) ∈ 𝒵_M for every polynomial p, and
   𝒮(t) ∈ 𝒵_M[[t]].
2. Δ ∈ 𝒵_𝒰.
3. For every eigenvalue θ, with spectral idempotents E_θ of A and E'_θ of A':
   E'_θ − E_θ ∈ 𝒵_M ⊗ ℝ. In words: **every spectral idempotent has a tied diagonal and a tied
   restriction to M**. This is the per-vertex spectral measure of the angle, for e_v, for 1
   and for e_v + 1 at once.

Argument. Diagonal: checked Theorem H, c_k tied. (A'^k − A^k) w_j = w^K_{k+j} − w_{k+j} = 0 by
Theorem H, w tied. 2 adds round-14 T1 (checked helper `nbr_bal`). 3: E_θ is a polynomial in A
and the same polynomial in A' gives E'_θ (equal spectra). ∎

So Theorem D's "Δ = P_𝒩 Δ P_𝒩" extends to the whole algebra: the linear map
Λ : p ↦ p(A') − p(A) takes values in 𝒵_M.

### 2.2 The block partition of the spectrum (paper)

I := ker Λ = {p : p(A') = p(A)} contains 1 and the minimal polynomial, and it is closed under
products. So I / (m_A) is a unital subalgebra of ℝ[x]/(m_A) ≅ ℝ^{spec A}, hence it is the
algebra of functions constant on the blocks of a partition ℬ of spec A (a unital subalgebra
of ℝ^X is spanned by its primitive idempotents). Therefore:

- E_β := Σ_{θ∈β} E_θ = E'_β for every block β; Δ commutes with every E_β and vanishes on every
  singleton block. **K is obtained from G by re-mixing eigenspaces inside the non-singleton
  blocks**, and only their non-main parts.
- **#spec A − #ℬ = rank Λ ≤ dim 𝒵_M.**
- dim 𝒵_M = 0 gives Δ = 0. If dim 𝒵_M = 1 and Δ ≠ 0, there is exactly one non-singleton block
  {θ, θ'}, A'^k − A^k = g_k Δ for scalars g_k (g_1 = 1), g_2 = θ + θ' is an **integer**, and
  A'² − g_2 A' = A² − g_2 A: every switched pair changes its codegree by exactly ±g_2, every
  other pair keeps its codegree, and Δ preserves (E_θ ⊕ E_{θ'}) ∩ 𝒩 and vanishes on its
  orthogonal complement.

This holds for any two cospectral symmetric matrices; the deck enters only through the bound
by dim 𝒵_M. It is description: I have no way to exclude a block of size 2 (strongly regular
graphs with equal parameters realise p(A') = p(A) for a quadratic p with K ≇ G, round-14 t2
§7.2).

### 2.3 Class ZD (zero-diagonal rigidity)

**ZD:** 𝒵_𝒰(G) = 0, i.e. the only symmetric integer matrix S with zero diagonal, S w_k = 0 for
all k and S 1_{deg=j} = 0 for all j is S = 0.

**Theorem ZD (paper).** Every graph in ZD is reconstructible.
Argument. Δ ∈ 𝒵_𝒰 by Proposition Z.2, so Δ = 0 and σ is an isomorphism. ∎ (The checked DWC₁
proof already constructs r := A' − A with `hsym`, `hdiag`, `hr1`, `hr2`; ZD applies its
hypothesis to r instead of to two rows of r.)

Linear-algebra reading. Let r = dim 𝒰^⊥ and B an n × r matrix whose columns are a basis of
𝒰^⊥, β_v ∈ ℝ^r its rows (the coordinates of P e_v). Every S ∈ 𝒵_𝒰 is BΛBᵀ with Λ a symmetric
r × r matrix and β_vᵀΛβ_v = 0 for all v. So

    ZD  ⇔  the n rank-one forms β_v β_vᵀ span Sym²(ℝ^r)  (dimension r(r + 1)/2).

- r ≤ 1: always (Λ = λ, λβ_v² = 0 for all v, B ≠ 0). So **DWC₁ ⊆ ZD**.
- r = 2: ZD fails iff the points β_v lie on at most two lines through 0, iff 𝒰^⊥ contains two
  nonzero vectors a, b with disjoint supports; then 𝒵_𝒰 = ℝ·(abᵀ + baᵀ). (A nonzero binary
  quadratic form vanishing at all β_v is neither definite nor of rank one, because B is
  injective; so it is a product of two independent linear forms.)
- any r: two nonzero vectors of 𝒰^⊥ with disjoint supports give abᵀ + baᵀ ∈ 𝒵_𝒰, so ZD fails.
  In particular ZD fails for a graph with two disjoint twin pairs, although those are
  harmless. Necessary for ZD: r(r + 1)/2 ≤ n.
- ZD ⊆ TW (Lemma SH.3).

Variant ZT (the ceiling of all linear information): the same with the extra hypotheses
S_xy ∈ {0, −1} on edges and S_xy ∈ {0, 1} on non-edges. It repairs the twin-pair defect and its
argument is the same line. But S ↦ G + S is a bijection onto graphs, so ZT is "every graph
with the walk vectors and neighbour-degree counts of G, in place, equals G": a quantifier
over graphs in disguise. I do not propose it; ZD is the honest linear class.

## 3. Corank 2: every Theorem-H tie is automatic (paper)

Assume dim 𝒩 = 2 and Δ ≠ 0. By §2.3 (with M in place of 𝒰) Δ = abᵀ + baᵀ with a, b spanning 𝒩,
supports X, Y disjoint, and after scaling a ∈ {±1}^X, b ∈ {±1}^Y: G has all edges X⁺–Y⁻, X⁻–Y⁺
and none X⁺–Y⁺, X⁻–Y⁻; K has the opposite; |X⁺| = |X⁻| = m, |Y⁺| = |Y⁻|. This is the balanced
block switch of the first diameter-2 sketch (S6; Zhu–Yue, arXiv:2609.36895, per
`notes/literature/novelty_open_core_lemmas.md` row 6b). Three additions:

1. **A' = RAR for the reflection R = I − aaᵀ/m, and N^K − N = g(t)·Δ with a scalar series g.**
   Argument: 𝒩 is A-invariant, Aa = pa + qb, and (Aa)_y = Σ_{x∈X, x~y} a_x = −m·b_y, so q = −m.
   Then RAR = A − (a(Aa)ᵀ + (Aa)aᵀ)/m + (aᵀAa)aaᵀ/m² = A − (q/m)(abᵀ + baᵀ) = A + Δ. The same
   computation with the resolvent (Na = αa + βb) gives RNR = N − (β/m)Δ. ∎
   So the diagonal of N^K − N vanishes identically and N^K − N kills M: **for a block switch
   with A-invariant span(a, b) ⊆ 1^⊥ all four conclusions of Theorem H hold whether or not the
   two graphs are hypomorphic.** The closed-walk half of Theorem H adds nothing at corank 2.
   (This is the reflection form of the switching of Wang–Qiu–Hu, *Cospectral graphs,
   GM-switching and regular rational orthogonal matrices of level p*, Linear Algebra Appl. 2019,
   doi:10.1016/j.laa.2018.10.027; only the index record was seen, theorems UNVERIFIED.
   Godsil–McKay switching: C. D. Godsil, B. D. McKay, *Constructing cospectral graphs*,
   Aequationes Math. 25 (1982) 257–268, from memory, UNVERIFIED.)
2. **If |X| = 2 or |Y| = 2 then K ≅ G** (a transposition). Argument: X = {x, x'}; (Aa)_z =
   [z ~ x] − [z ~ x'] vanishes off X ∪ Y by invariance, so N(x) and N(x') agree off X ∪ Y and
   are complementary on Y; K = G^{(x x')}. ∎ More generally: **dim 𝒩 = 2 and e_x − e_x' ∈ 𝒩 for
   some x ≠ x' imply reconstructible** (with d := 1_{N(x)∖x'} − 1_{N(x')∖x}: if d = 0 the twins
   force Δ = 0 by the sign pattern or by three lines; if d ≠ 0 then 𝒩 = span{e_x − e_x', d}
   and Δ ∈ {0, −(e_x − e_x')dᵀ − d(e_x − e_x')ᵀ}, the second being G^{(x x')}). By Lemma RS this
   covers every graph of walk corank 2 with two removal-similar vertices.
3. Otherwise (|X|, |Y| ≥ 4) all n cards are pairwise non-isomorphic (a removal-similar pair
   would put a vector with two nonzero entries into span(a, b)), and for every v ∉ X ∪ Y the
   card isomorphism f_v preserves the card type of every vertex. No contradiction follows.

Item 2 is a small class ("walk corank 2 with a vertex difference in 𝒩"). Its statement needs
"dim 𝒩 ≤ 2" over integer vectors (any three are dependent); I do not propose it as a child:
ZD already contains the three-line case and the rest is a transposition.

## 4. The off-diagonal data: Lemma PAIR and the transfer relation

### 4.1 Lemma PAIR (paper; entries of the checked identity `alpha`)

The checked identity (α): C_v·(N − N₀ + E) = N E N (Lean: `alpha`) at the entries (x, x) and
(x, ·)1, x ≠ v, using Aᵀ = A:

    (P1)  C_v·(w_x − w_x^{(v)}) = N_xv·w_v,        (P2)  C_v·(C_x − C_x^{(v)}) = N_xv².

Write them for K (whose types are those of G by Theorem H) and transport the card by f := f_v,
y := f(x): w_x^{K,(v)} = w_y^{(v)} and C_x^{K,(v)} = C_y^{(v)}. Subtracting, with a := N^K_xv and
b := N^G_yv:

    (E1)  C_v·(w_x − w_y) = w_v·(a − b),           (E2)  C_v·(C_x − C_y) = a² − b².

Consequences (all series are those of G unless marked K):

- **Displacement.** w^{(v)}_{f_v x} − w^{(v)}_x = −λ_v·𝒮_xv. Row v of N^K − N^G is, up to the unit
  −λ_v, the displacement of f_v on the walk series of the card G − v. At order t this is
  z_v(x) = d^{(v)}(x) − d^{(v)}(f_v x) of §1.1.
- **Pair criterion.** By (P2) for K and for G at the same x: N^K_xv = N^G_xv iff
  C_x^{K,(v)} = C_x^{(v)} (x has the same closed-walk series in K − v and in G − v). At order t²
  this reads Δ_xv = 0 iff deg_{K−v} x = deg_{G−v} x iff e(K − v − x) = e(G − v − x). So "Δ = 0" is
  "the two-vertex-deleted cards are aligned at the level of edge counts", and the deck
  supplies them only as a multiset per apex: K − v − x ≅ G − v − f_v(x). (In characteristic-polynomial form this is Jacobi's identity
  N_xx N_vv − N_xv² ↔ φ(G − x − v)/φ(G); from memory, UNVERIFIED, not used.)
- **Tied multiset.** For each v the multiset over x ≠ v of
  (w_x − λ_v N_xv, C_x − N_xv²/C_v) is the same for N = N^G and N = N^K (it is the multiset of
  card types). Its power sums of order 2 return Theorem H ((N²)_vv and (N²1)_v); from order 3
  on they are single equations between several unknown sums (Σ_x C_x N_xv, Σ_x w_x N_xv²,
  Σ_x N_xv³), in agreement with round-14 t3's master system.
- **Three-point form** (walks avoiding a third vertex): N^K_xy − N^K_xv N^K_vy / C_v =
  N^G_{fx,fy} − N^G_{fx,v} N^G_{v,fy} / C_v for x, y ≠ v. It is the card isomorphism at resolvent
  level and carries no alignment.

### 4.2 The transfer relation

Eliminate a from (E1), (E2) (multiply the claim by C_v; ℤ[[t]] is a domain):

    (TR)   2·N_yv·w_v·(w_x − w_y) = w_v²·(C_x − C_y) − C_v·(w_x − w_y)².

**Lemma TR (paper).** For an aligned hypomorph, every v and every x ≠ v, the vertex
y = f_v(x) satisfies (TR), **an identity between walk series of G alone**. Moreover
N^K_xv = N_yv + (C_v/w_v)(w_x − w_y).

Write x →_v y when (TR) holds; x →_v x always, and →_v contains the graph of every card
isomorphism of every aligned hypomorph, in particular (K = G) of every automorphism of
G − v and (Lemma SH) of the card isomorphisms that come from removal-similar vertices.

Reading. If w_x ≠ w_y then (TR) **determines** the off-diagonal series:

    N_yv = ½·[ w_v(C_x − C_y)/(w_x − w_y) − (C_v/w_v)(w_x − w_y) ],

a function of the three types τ_v, τ_x, τ_y only. If w_x = w_y then (TR) forces C_x = C_y and
says nothing about N_yv. So: *a vertex y that a card isomorphism at v reaches from a vertex of
another type has its whole walk series to v dictated by per-vertex data; a vertex reached
from its own type is unconstrained.* This is the exact content of "the per-vertex spectral
measures agree" for off-diagonal entries.

Low orders of (TR), with d = degree, s = w₂ (sum of neighbour degrees), t = triangles through
the vertex, κ = codeg(y, v), ε := d_x − d_y:
- t²: ε ∈ {0, 1, −1}, and ε = 1 − 2[y ~ v] when ε ≠ 0.
- t³, ε = −1 (y ~ v):  κ = t_y − t_x.
- t³, ε = +1 (y ≁ v):  κ = d_v + (t_x − s_x) − (t_y − s_y).
Direct derivation of the t³ lines without series (used in §5): for ε = −1, x ≁_K v, so the
triangles at x in K − v are t_x, and at y in G − v they are t_y − κ. For ε = +1, x ~_K v:
t_x − codeg_K(x, v) = t_y and (s_x − d_v) − codeg_K(x, v) = s_y − κ.

### 4.3 Hand check of (TR) on P₄ (the same graph as §1.2)

Vertices a, u, u', b; D := det(I − tA) = 1 − 3t² + t⁴ = (1 + t − t²)(1 − t − t²), q := 1 − t − t².
w_a = w_b = 1/q, w_u = w_{u'} = (1 + t)/q, C_a = C_b = (1 − 2t²)/D, C_u = C_{u'} = (1 − t²)/D,
N_ba = t³/D. For K = G^{(u u')} take v = a and the card isomorphism f_a : u ↦ u', u' ↦ b, b ↦ u
(K − a is the path u'–u–b, G − a the path u–u'–b). For (x, y) = (u', b):
left side 2·(t³/D)·(1/q)·(t/q) = 2t⁴/(Dq²); right side (1/q²)(t²/D) − ((1 − 2t²)/D)(t²/q²) =
2t⁴/(Dq²). ✓. And N^K_{u'a} = t³/D + (C_a/w_a)(t/q) = (t³ + t − 2t³)/D = t(1 − t²)/D, the series of
an end vertex and its neighbour, as it must be (u' ~_K a). ✓. Order t³: κ = codeg(b, a) = 0 =
d_a + (t_{u'} − s_{u'}) − (t_b − s_b) = 1 + (0 − 3) − (0 − 2). ✓

### 4.4 Sums over degree classes

⟨1_{deg=i}, A^k 1_{deg=j}⟩ is tied for k ≤ 2: k = 1 is T1, and k = 2 is ⟨A1_{deg=i}, A1_{deg=j}⟩, an
inner product of two tied vectors. k = 3 is ⟨m_i, A m_j⟩ with m_j := A1_{deg=j}, i.e.
m_iᵀΔm_j = 0, the first instance of round-14 t3's "rooted Kelly" at G-level. A Kelly count of
induced copies of a pattern F with one vertex a labelled by its G-degree gives, for
x_j := number of copies in which a has j neighbours outside the copy and L := n − |F|, the
equations (L − j)·x_j + (j + 1)·x_{j+1} = (deck sum), j < L. Their kernel is x_j = (−1)^j·binom(L, j),
and the total count does not remove it. So such numbers are tied only up to the count of
copies in which the labelled vertex is adjacent to everything (or to nothing) outside the
copy. (Derived once, not cross-checked.) I have no argument for k = 3.
In aligned form (A^k 1_{deg=j})_v is tied for k ≤ 1 only.

## 5. Classes from the transfer relation

**TRR (transfer-rigid apex):** some v such that x →_v y implies deg x = deg y.
**Theorem TRR (paper).** Every graph in TRR is reconstructible.
Argument. By Lemma TR, f_v preserves G-degrees; with deg_{K−v} x = deg_{G−v} f_v x this gives
x ~_K v ⇔ f_v x ~_G v, so f_v extends by v ↦ v (checked helper `iso_of_nbhd`). ∎

Since Σ_y δ_v(y) = 0, a non-extending f_v has both a y with δ_v(y) = −1 and one with +1. So one
half suffices, and the order-3 truncation is already a class:

**TRR₃⁻:** some v such that there are no y ~ v and x ≠ v with deg x = deg y − 1 and
t_x = t_y − codeg(v, y).
**TRR₃⁺:** some v such that there are no y ≁ v, y ≠ v, and x ≠ v with deg x = deg y + 1 and
codeg(v, y) = d_v + (t_x − s_x) − (t_y − s_y).
**Theorem TRR₃ (paper).** Every graph in TRR₃⁻ ∪ TRR₃⁺ is reconstructible.
Argument. §4.2, direct derivation; needs deg, s and t tied (checked `deg_T0`, `w2_tied`, and
c₃ from `hagos_induction`; t alone is also Kelly for K₃). ∎

In words, TRR₃⁻: *no vertex of G has the degree and the triangle count that some neighbour of
v has after v is deleted.* The classical criterion "N(v) is a union of card-degree classes"
compares a neighbour with a non-neighbour of v inside the card; TRR₃ compares a card vertex
with **any** vertex of G through G-level tied data, so the two are not comparable by an
argument I have. Necessary conditions for the residual after carving: at **every** vertex v
there are y ~ v, x with (d_x, t_x) = (d_y − 1, t_y − codeg(v, y)), and y' ≁ v, x' with the
mirrored equation.

Relation to WR. With T_v := {y : x →_v y for some x with deg x ≠ deg y} (transfer targets),
supp δ_v ⊆ T_v. So **WR⁺** := "WR with the extra hypothesis supp δ ⊆ T_v on δ" contains both WR
and TRR and is still a property of G alone that quantifies over integer vectors only. The
ceiling of everything in this section is the class TMR: "the only S ⊆ V ∖ v whose
re-attachment has the type multiset of G (and the type of G at v) is S = N(v)"; its conditions
on δ = 1_S − 1_N imply those of WR (sum the lifted walk series; compare degree histograms).
TMR needs the walk series of a second graph or their convolution formulas in the statement,
so it is not proposed.

Blind spot (the same as WR's): TRR fails at v as soon as G − v has an automorphism moving
N(v), or v has a removal-similar mate that is not similar to it, because →_v contains those
maps. On such graphs only an "up to Sym(𝒫)" statement can work (§1.2).

## 6. Why there is no trace or rank contradiction at this level

1. Every identity obtainable from Theorem H, symmetry, zero diagonal and the entry pattern
   is satisfied by Δ = P_τᵀAP_τ − A, τ ∈ Sym(𝒫) (Lemma SH; P₄). So no such identity gives Δ = 0,
   and tr(Δ²) = 2|E(G) △ E(K)| > 0 is consistent with all of them.
2. The suggested identity: X := A'² − A² = AΔ + ΔA + Δ² ∈ 𝒵_M. Its diagonal is
   2(AΔ)_vv + (Δ²)_vv = −2·lost(v) + lost(v) + gained(v), i.e. lost = gained at v, already
   contained in Δ1 = 0. Its trace is 0 = 2tr(AΔ) + tr(Δ²), the same statement summed. Along
   the segment A + εΔ the walk vectors are constant and (A_ε²)_vv = d_v − 2ε(1 − ε)·lost(v): the
   two endpoints are tied and nothing else is.
3. Higher traces tr(A'^k) = tr(A^k) and their diagonals are Kelly counts of closed walks; at
   corank 2 they hold identically for every block switch (§3.1), and for two strongly
   regular graphs with equal parameters under any bijection (round-14 t2 §7.2), where K ≇ G.
4. Rank: rank Δ ≤ dim 𝒰^⊥ and the rows of Δ are {0, ±1}-vectors of 𝒰^⊥, so Δ has at most
   3^r distinct rows (a vector of an r-dimensional space is determined by r coordinates).
   This gives twin classes of Δ when |supp Δ| > 3^r, and no contradiction.
5. What is not used by any of the above is that each f_v is a **permutation**: the multiset
   statement of §4.1 and its pointwise form (TR). A contradiction, if there is one, has to
   come from the incompatibility of the n relations →_v with one matrix Δ: for a switched
   pair {x, v} both f_v(x) and f_x(v) are constrained, N^K_xv = N_{f_v x, v} + (C_v/w_v)(w_x −
   w_{f_v x}) = N_{f_x v, x} + (C_x/w_x)(w_v − w_{f_x v}). I found no way to close this system;
   it is the pair census of the earlier rounds written with resolvents.
