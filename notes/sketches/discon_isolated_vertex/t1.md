# Sketch t1: discon_isolated_vertex, degree route

Thinker t1. This is a sketch, not a result. Only `gate.py check` decides.

Statement: `Recon.ClassReconstructible (fun n G => ∃ v : Fin n, ∀ w : Fin n, ¬ G.Adj v w)`.
Unfolded: for n ≥ 3, G on `Fin n` with a vertex v that has no neighbour, and any H on `Fin n`
with a bijection σ such that `card G x ≃g card H (σ x)` for every x, there is an
isomorphism `G ≃g H`.

Step record on 2026-11-25: frozen, approved, two fidelity passes, `dead_ends: []`,
no earlier sketches. Nothing to avoid.

## 0. One correction to the assigned angle

The angle says "kelly_degree_sequence gives a vertex u of H with degree 0". Do not route
through `kelly_degree_sequence`, for two reasons.

1. It is **frozen, not checked** (`desk.py status kelly_degree_sequence`), so a proof file
   may not import it (gate rule 1: only already-checked `Recon.Checked.*`).
2. Its conclusion is `∃ σ' : Fin n ≃ Fin n, ∀ v, deg_G v = deg_H (σ' v)` with a **fresh**
   σ'. That σ' is not tied to the card isomorphisms. It would give some isolated u in H but
   no isomorphism `G − v ≅ H − u`, and the extension step needs exactly that.

What the route needs is the *pointwise* identity for the hypomorphism σ itself, at the one
vertex v: `deg_H (σ v) = deg_G v`. That is three lines of arithmetic from the two checked
steps below. (The same identity for all x is also the natural proof of
`kelly_degree_sequence`, with witness σ' := σ.)

## 1. Route

Fix n ≥ 3, G, the isolated vertex v, H, σ and the card isomorphisms. Let w := σ v and pick
`e : card G v ≃g card H w`.

**Step A (degree transfer; all in ℕ, no subtraction).**

- (A1) `e(G − v) + deg_G v = e(G)`: `L_kelly_card_edges_add_degree n G v`.
- (A2) `e(H − w) + deg_H w = e(H)`: `L_kelly_card_edges_add_degree n H (σ v)`.
- (A3) `e(G) = e(H)`: `L_kelly_edge_count n hn G H ⟨σ, hσ⟩`. This is the only use of n ≥ 3.
- (A4) `e(G − v) = e(H − w)`: isomorphic graphs have equally many edges; the helper
  `Recon.Checked.L_kelly_edge_count.ncard_eq_of_iso e` is already in the checked library.
- (A5) `deg_G v = 0`: `G.neighborSet v = ∅` because v is isolated.

`omega` on A1–A5 gives `(H.neighborSet w).ncard = 0`. Since `Fin n` is finite,
`Set.ncard_eq_zero` turns this into `H.neighborSet w = ∅`, that is `∀ y, ¬ H.Adj w y`.
The finiteness matters: `ncard` of an infinite set is 0 by convention.

**Step B (extension lemma; no finiteness, no `Fin`, no n ≥ 3).**

> For any types V, W, graphs G on V and H on W, v with no G-neighbour and w with no
> H-neighbour: an isomorphism `e : card G v ≃g card H w` extends to `G ≃g H`.

Define `f x := if x = v then w else e ⟨x, _⟩` and `g y := if y = w then v else e.symm ⟨y, _⟩`.
- `g ∘ f = id`: if x = v both branches are the first; otherwise `f x = e ⟨x,_⟩` has value
  ≠ w (it lives in `{w}ᶜ`), so `g (f x) = e.symm (e ⟨x,_⟩) = x`. Symmetric for `f ∘ g`.
- Adjacency `H.Adj (f a) (f b) ↔ G.Adj a b`: if a = v both sides are false (w and v are
  isolated); if b = v the same after `Adj.symm`; otherwise it is `e.map_rel_iff`, because
  adjacency in `card` is adjacency in the ambient graph by definition (`induce` is `comap`
  of the subtype embedding, so the statement is closed by `Iff` up to definitional
  unfolding).

Step A + Step B give `Nonempty (G ≃g H)`.

## 3. Plumbing a builder needs

- **The subtype.** `card G v : SimpleGraph ({v}ᶜ : Set (Fin n))`. An element is `⟨x, h⟩`
  with `h : x ∈ ({v}ᶜ : Set _)`. A hypothesis `h : ¬ x = v` is accepted directly by the
  anonymous constructor (membership unfolds to it definitionally); no `simp` is needed.
  Likewise `(e ⟨x, h⟩).2 : ¬ (e ⟨x, h⟩ : W) = w` type-checks as is.
- **Do not unfold `f` inside the goals.** State four rewriting facts once
  (`f v = w`, `g w = v`, `f x = e ⟨x, h⟩` for `h : ¬ x = v`, and the same for g) with
  `dite_eq_left` / `dite_eq_right` (`dif_pos` / `dif_neg` are deprecated in this Mathlib
  and warn), then use only those. This avoids motive problems with dependent `if`.
- **Subtype round trip.** To rewrite `e.symm ⟨f x, h2⟩`, first prove
  `(⟨f x, h2⟩ : ({w}ᶜ : Set W)) = e ⟨x, h⟩` by `Subtype.ext`, then
  `RelIso.symm_apply_apply`.
- **Building the iso.** `⟨{ toEquiv := ⟨f, g, hgf, hfg⟩, map_rel_iff' := ?_ }⟩`, then
  `change H.Adj (f a) (f b) ↔ G.Adj a b` to get rid of the `Equiv` coercion.
- **Classical.** `classical` at the top of the extension lemma supplies `Decidable (x = v)`
  for general V. No `DecidableRel G.Adj` is needed anywhere: both reused statements are
  phrased with `Set.ncard`.
- **Degree form.** Stay with `(G.neighborSet v).ncard`; do not convert to `G.degree`.
  `Set.eq_empty_of_forall_notMem` + `Set.ncard_empty` for A5, and
  `Set.ncard_eq_zero (Set.toFinite _)` for the way back.
- **Arithmetic.** `omega` closes A1–A5 → `(H.neighborSet (σ v)).ncard = 0` once the five
  facts are hypotheses with syntactically equal atoms. They are, because `Recon.card` is
  an `abbrev` and the checked statements use it literally.
- **Alternative constructions, not recommended.** `Equiv.Set.sumCompl` with
  `Equiv.Set.singleton`, or `Equiv.optionSubtypeNe` (its subtype is `{b // b ≠ a}`, which is
  only definitionally, not syntactically, `({a}ᶜ : Set _)`). Both make the adjacency proof
  go through more coercion lemmas than the direct `dite`.

### Mathlib API (present in the pinned Mathlib; file under `lean/.lake/packages/mathlib/Mathlib/`)

- `dite_eq_left`, `dite_eq_right` (core/Mathlib logic)
- `Subtype.ext`, `RelIso.symm_apply_apply`, `RelIso.apply_symm_apply`, `RelIso.map_rel_iff`
- `SimpleGraph.Iso` as `RelIso` (`toEquiv`, `map_rel_iff'`), `SimpleGraph.induce`,
  `SimpleGraph.comap_adj` (`Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Maps.lean:136`; not needed explicitly)
- `SimpleGraph.mem_neighborSet` (`Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Basic.lean:808`; membership is
  definitional, so `hv x hx` works directly)
- `Set.eq_empty_of_forall_notMem`, `Set.ncard_empty`,
  `Set.ncard_eq_zero` (`Data/Set/Card.lean:697`), `Set.toFinite`
- Optional restatement in Mathlib vocabulary: `SimpleGraph.IsIsolated`
  (`Combinatorics/SimpleGraph/Basic.lean:1038`, `G.IsIsolated v := ∀ w, ¬ G.Adj v w`, which is
  the class predicate verbatim) and `SimpleGraph.neighborSet_eq_empty` (`:1040`).

## 4. Lean probe (API check only, not a verdict)

To make sure the API names and the definitional steps above are real, I elaborated the
following file with `cd lean && lake env lean <file>` from my scratchpad (outside the repo).
It elaborated without errors or warnings, and a `#print axioms` probe on a copy listed only
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`. **I did not run the gate** (precheck or check);
shape, forbidden-token and isolation checks are untested. A builder should copy it to
`lean/Recon/Scratch/discon_isolated_vertex/b1.lean` and run `gate.py precheck`.

```lean
import Mathlib
import Recon.Statements.S_discon_isolated_vertex
import Recon.Checked.L_kelly_edge_count
import Recon.Checked.L_kelly_card_edges_add_degree

namespace Recon.Checked.L_discon_isolated_vertex

open SimpleGraph

lemma iso_of_card_iso_of_isolated {V W : Type*} {G : SimpleGraph V} {H : SimpleGraph W}
    {v : V} {w : W} (hv : ∀ x, ¬ G.Adj v x) (hw : ∀ y, ¬ H.Adj w y)
    (e : Recon.card G v ≃g Recon.card H w) : Nonempty (G ≃g H) := by
  classical
  let f : V → W := fun x => if h : x = v then w else (e ⟨x, h⟩ : W)
  let g : W → V := fun y => if h : y = w then v else (e.symm ⟨y, h⟩ : V)
  have hf : ∀ x (h : ¬ x = v), f x = (e ⟨x, h⟩ : W) := fun x h => dite_eq_right h
  have hg : ∀ y (h : ¬ y = w), g y = (e.symm ⟨y, h⟩ : V) := fun y h => dite_eq_right h
  have hfv : f v = w := dite_eq_left rfl
  have hgw : g w = v := dite_eq_left rfl
  have hgf : ∀ x, g (f x) = x := by
    intro x
    by_cases h : x = v
    · subst h; rw [hfv, hgw]
    · have h2 : ¬ f x = w := by rw [hf x h]; exact (e ⟨x, h⟩).2
      rw [hg _ h2]
      have : (⟨f x, h2⟩ : ({w}ᶜ : Set W)) = e ⟨x, h⟩ := Subtype.ext (hf x h)
      rw [this, RelIso.symm_apply_apply]
  have hfg : ∀ y, f (g y) = y := by
    intro y
    by_cases h : y = w
    · subst h; rw [hgw, hfv]
    · have h2 : ¬ g y = v := by rw [hg y h]; exact (e.symm ⟨y, h⟩).2
      rw [hf _ h2]
      have : (⟨g y, h2⟩ : ({v}ᶜ : Set V)) = e.symm ⟨y, h⟩ := Subtype.ext (hg y h)
      rw [this, RelIso.apply_symm_apply]
  refine ⟨{ toEquiv := ⟨f, g, hgf, hfg⟩, map_rel_iff' := ?_ }⟩
  intro a b
  change H.Adj (f a) (f b) ↔ G.Adj a b
  by_cases ha : a = v
  · subst ha
    rw [hfv]
    exact ⟨fun h => (hw _ h).elim, fun h => (hv _ h).elim⟩
  · by_cases hb : b = v
    · subst hb
      rw [hfv]
      exact ⟨fun h => (hw _ h.symm).elim, fun h => (hv _ h.symm).elim⟩
    · rw [hf a ha, hf b hb]
      exact e.map_rel_iff (a := ⟨a, ha⟩) (b := ⟨b, hb⟩)

end Recon.Checked.L_discon_isolated_vertex

theorem Recon.Checked.L_discon_isolated_vertex : Recon.Statements.S_discon_isolated_vertex := by
  intro n hn G ⟨v, hv⟩ H ⟨σ, hσ⟩
  obtain ⟨e⟩ := hσ v
  have hE := Recon.Checked.L_kelly_edge_count n hn G H ⟨σ, hσ⟩
  have hG := Recon.Checked.L_kelly_card_edges_add_degree n G v
  have hH := Recon.Checked.L_kelly_card_edges_add_degree n H (σ v)
  have hc := Recon.Checked.L_kelly_edge_count.ncard_eq_of_iso e
  have hGv : (G.neighborSet v).ncard = 0 := by
    have h0 : G.neighborSet v = ∅ := Set.eq_empty_of_forall_notMem (fun x hx => hv x hx)
    rw [h0, Set.ncard_empty]
  have hHv : (H.neighborSet (σ v)).ncard = 0 := by omega
  have hw : ∀ y, ¬ H.Adj (σ v) y := by
    intro y hy
    rw [Set.ncard_eq_zero (Set.toFinite _)] at hHv
    have hy' : y ∈ H.neighborSet (σ v) := hy
    rw [hHv] at hy'
    exact hy'
  exact Recon.Checked.L_discon_isolated_vertex.iso_of_card_iso_of_isolated hv hw e
```

## 5. Small-graph tests

Claim file: `notes/sketches/discon_isolated_vertex/t1_claim.py`.

```
python3 tools/search/small_graphs.py counterexample \
    --claim notes/sketches/discon_isolated_vertex/t1_claim.py --n-min 3 --n-max 7
NO WITNESS after 2498 checks (exhaustive n=3..7, random n=[])
```

It tests, on every graph with 3 ≤ n ≤ 7:
- **D**: `deg v = e(G) − e(G − v)` (A1/A2);
- **X**: the extension lemma in the form "if v is isolated in G, every H on n vertices with
  an isolated w and `H − w ≅ G − v` is isomorphic to G" (Step B);
- **P** (on same-deck pairs): whenever `G − x ≅ H − y`, `deg_G x = deg_H y` (Step A, in a
  stronger form than needed).

Caveat on P: for n ≤ 7 every deck class is a single isomorphism class, so P is only
exercised with H ≅ G. It still tests "isomorphic cards of one graph have equal degree".

Negative control, `--n-min 2 --n-max 2`: WITNESS, G = 2K₁ and H = K₂ (P fails). This is the
`Basic.lean` anchor and marks where `3 ≤ n` enters (A3).

## 6. Where it could break

- **Paper level:** I see no gap. Step A is exact arithmetic on checked identities; Step B is
  a definition by cases. The only hypothesis used beyond the checked steps is that v is
  isolated.
- **A3 is load-bearing.** Without the same edge count, isomorphic cards do not force equal
  degrees: G = P₃ at a leaf and H = K₂ + K₁ at the isolated vertex both have the card K₂,
  with degrees 1 and 0. So the argument cannot be shortened to "the card at v is the card
  at σ v, hence σ v is isolated".
- **Lean level:** low risk given the probe. Remaining unknowns are gate-only: the
  one-line final `theorem` shape, the forbidden-token scan (the probe has no comments,
  no double quotes, no `#`), and that importing two `Recon.Checked.*` files is allowed
  (rule 1 says it is).
- **Fragility:** the probe depends on `⟨x, h⟩` with `h : ¬ x = v` being accepted for
  `({v}ᶜ : Set V)`. If a later Mathlib changes the unfolding, replace `h` by
  `(by simpa using h)`.

## 7. Sanity against the known failure settings

- **n = 2:** fails (2K₁ vs K₂); the route uses n ≥ 3 through `kelly_edge_count`. Consistent.
- **Infinite graphs (Fisher 1969, as listed in CLAUDE.md §3; not read by me):** Step A is
  finite counting (`ncard`, division by n − 2 inside `kelly_edge_count`) and has no
  infinite analogue. Step B alone holds for infinite graphs but needs σ v isolated, which
  is exactly what cannot be derived. Consistent.
- **Tournaments (Stockmeyer) and 3-uniform hypergraphs (Kocay), as listed in CLAUDE.md §3;
  not read by me:** the analogue of this argument would go through for a digraph or
  hypergraph with an isolated vertex (arc/edge counts are deck-determined for large enough
  n; paper remark, not tested here). That is no contradiction: a tournament on ≥ 2 vertices has no isolated vertex, and
  the argument says nothing about objects without one. UNVERIFIED that Kocay's hypergraph
  pairs have no isolated vertex; I did not check the paper.

## 8. Literature

The argument above is self-contained given the two checked steps; no literature result is
used as an ingredient. For attribution only:

- The statement is a special case of "disconnected graphs are reconstructible", attributed
  to P. J. Kelly, "A congruence theorem for trees", *Pacific J. Math.* 7 (1957) 961–968, in
  `notes/literature/structural_reductions.md` (candidate C5). I did not read the paper for
  this sketch; UNVERIFIED by me.
- The pointwise degree identity `deg_G x = deg_H (σ x)` is recorded as Kelly 1957, Lemma 2
  in `notes/literature/kelly_recognizable_invariants.md` §2, where the librarian reports
  having read the source. I rely on the checked Lean steps, not on that citation.

## 9. By-products worth proposing (for the strategist; not part of this step)

- The extension lemma (Step B) should generalise (paper remark, not tested here): an isomorphism of cards `G − v ≅ H − w` that
  maps `N_G(v)` onto `N_H(w)` extends to `G ≅ H`. That form is what the `regular` program
  needs (neighbours of the deleted vertex are the vertices of degree d − 1 in the card).
  The isolated case is the instance with both neighbourhoods empty.
- `kelly_degree_pointwise` (same σ) follows from Step A without the isolation hypothesis
  and implies the frozen `kelly_degree_sequence` with witness σ.
